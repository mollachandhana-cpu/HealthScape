What can I do now?

♡

General precautions selected from the leading indicators. Follow local public-health guidance for your situation.

Even without a current risk assessment, everyday prevention remains useful.

  • ✓Practical action
  • ✓Use safe drinking water and follow local water-quality advisories.
  • ✓Remove standing water where practical and keep water containers covered.
  • ✓Follow official weather and public-health guidance; seek professional care when unwell.

03 / FOR HEALTH OFFICERS

What should be verified?

⌖

A risk signal should lead to evidence review and accountable follow-up—not an automatic declaration of an outbreak.

  1. 01
    Validate the evidenceCheck timestamps, source, missing values and the field measurement method.
  2. 02
    Review community reportsCompare recent observations and confirm them with an authorized worker.
  3. 03
    Record the responseAssign follow-up, acknowledge alerts and document resolution.
