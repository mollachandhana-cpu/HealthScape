Environmental stress indicators

These signals describe different types of outdoor exposure.

☼
Heat strain

Use apparent temperature alongside actual temperature. Hydrate, seek shade and reschedule strenuous activity during hotter periods.

✳
UV exposure

When UV is high, use shade, protective clothing, sunglasses and sunscreen. UV can be elevated even when air temperature feels comfortable.

↘
Heavy rain & local disruption

Forecast rainfall is not a flood prediction. Check official warnings and avoid flooded roads or flowing water.

≋
Wind & low visibility

Elevated gusts can affect exposed areas and temporary structures. Low visibility may require slower, more cautious travel.
